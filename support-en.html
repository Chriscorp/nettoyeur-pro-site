Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install hardware in the store?
No. No probes, no tablet. Your team scans a QR code with the phone they already carry and enters the temperature. The QR code prints on plain A4 paper.
Does the app work without a network in the stockrooms?
Yes. Readings are taken offline and synchronise when the network returns. No data is lost.
How long does it take to set up a store?
Half a day : we list the store's cabinets, print the labels, stick them on, and train the team on site.
What happens if a reading is missed?
The app flags it as missing, with the expected date and time. That is exactly what a health inspection requires you to prove.
How do I delete my account and my data?
Write to contact@nettoyeur.pro from the account address. Deletion is completed within 30 days and confirmed in writing.
